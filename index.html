I am a Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at Yonsei University,
in the V-Lab advised by Prof. EunByung Park.

My research focuses on Perception and Robot Learning, with the goal of developing AI systems that can perceive, reason about, and interact with the real world using multimodal sensor data. I am particularly interested in spatial reasoning and in building frameworks that translate intelligent perception into intelligent robotic behavior for solving complex real-world problems.

Current Interests: Dexterous Manipulation, Human-Robot Integration

Previously, Research intern at RLLAB (Prof. Youngwoon Lee) and CSI Lab (Prof. Yusung Kim).
